Overview

Embodiment-aware editing, not generic style transfer

Egocentric human videos are abundant and diverse, but human and robot hands differ in appearance, geometry, articulation, kinematics, and viewpoint. A useful robot-centric edit must preserve the scene, object state, task semantics, and hand-object contact while instantiating the requested robotic morphology.

HandEdit addresses this missing evaluation problem with a unified dataset and benchmark spanning Hand-only and Hand-Arm tracks. The evaluation suite combines generic similarity, VLM-based judgment, and embodiment-aware metrics for removal, target fidelity, and interaction consistency.

Data curation

A kinematically grounded rendering-and-compositing pipeline

HandEdit removes the human region, restores the occluded background, retargets MANO or 3D hand pose to the target URDF, solves the arm configuration when needed, renders under the egocentric camera, and composites the robotic foreground back into the scene.

HandEdit Benchmark

Two evaluation tracks, both embodiment-aware

Each official track contains 1K test images paired with 13 corresponding target embodiments. Models are evaluated under their native conditioning interfaces with fixed prompt templates and standardized settings.

Track 01

Hand-only

Replace the local human hand region with a requested dexterous robot hand while preserving object state and hand-object interaction.

  • 13 standalone hand embodiments
  • Localized edited ROI
  • Strong morphology sensitivity
Track 02

Hand-Arm

Replace the visible human hand and forearm/full arm with a target hand-arm embodiment under larger-region scene constraints.

  • 13 hand-arm embodiments
  • Larger background recovery region
  • Arm kinematics and composition
Evaluation stack

Measure what generic image quality misses

HandEdit combines complementary signals so a visually plausible but embodiment-wrong edit cannot dominate the benchmark.

01

Generic Similarity

PSNR · SSIM · LPIPS · FID on full image, ROI, and background where applicable.

02

VLM-based Judgment

Semantic Consistency (SC) and Perceptual Quality (PQ), combined into a normalized VLM score.

03

Embodiment-Aware Metrics

Human-hand Removal · Structural Fidelity · ID Fidelity · Interaction Consistency.

Finding 01

GPT-Image-2 is the strongest overall baseline

It leads key local editing and embodiment-aware measures, including structural fidelity and interaction preservation.

Finding 02

VLM judgment is useful but not sufficient

High semantic/perceptual scores do not always align with fine-grained morphology or contact-level interaction preservation.

Finding 03

Embodiment-aware editing remains the core challenge

Human-hand removal is comparatively easier; structural fidelity, robot identity, and interaction show larger gaps.
